### Headline number has an independent ground truth

nDCG@10 lift, E1 minus E0, with a five-seed band, on held-out customers' recorded purchases in the held-out window. The retailer recorded those purchases, so the build does not produce them. SPEC.md keeps heldout customers out of training in both windows, tunes on dev only, and states that a band crossing zero is no lift. No leakage in the split rule as written: training uses data from before T_dev, and the candidate pool uses the 12 weeks before the window.
