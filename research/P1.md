## 4. Competitor comparison

Two closest competitors for "taste" in a generative image product: Midjourney (the only one found that ships behaviour-learned personalization) and Ideogram (a community feed plus custom models, like Krea).

| Capability | Krea | Midjourney | Ideogram |
|---|---|---|---|
| Community feed | Gallery | Explore page | Explore, Following, category filters |
| Personal taste from behaviour | No | **Yes**: Personalization profiles (`--p`). "By liking or selecting images, Midjourney gets to know what kind of aesthetics you love"; "Liking images from other members on the Explore page will influence your Global Profiles." | No: likes go to Library → Favorites; no taste model described |
| Explicit style set | Moodboards (with taste profile) | Moodboards | Style reference images (up to 10), 62 style presets |
| Trained per-user model | Krea 2 LoRAs | not read | Custom models (15 to 100 images) |
| Feed ranked per viewer | No evidence | No evidence (personalization steers generation, not the feed) | No evidence (Explore / Following) |

Sources: Midjourney `https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/32433330574221-Personalization`; Ideogram `https://docs.ideogram.ai/get-started/tour`, `https://docs.ideogram.ai/create/custom-models`, `https://docs.ideogram.ai/create/style-reference`, `https://docs.ideogram.ai/llms-full.txt`.

**Where the build sits.** Midjourney applies a behaviour-learned taste profile to generation; none of the three publishes a per-viewer ranking of a feed, or any offline evaluation of one. taste-rank learns taste from a user's history and ranks a catalogue for that user, measured on that user's later behaviour. It is not a copy of Midjourney's `--p` (which conditions the generator); it is the ranking and evaluation half the Krea posting describes ("curated feeds", "ranking", "aesthetic evaluation"). The data is fashion e-commerce, not generated images, so the transfer to a generated-image feed is an argument, labelled as such in P6.

## 6. Three candidates, scored (BUILD_OUTLINE.md section 2 rubric)

**A. taste-rank (the brief's build).** LoRA fine-tune of a fashion image-text encoder so that a customer's purchase history, embedded, ranks the articles they buy next. E0 zero-shot, E1 LoRA, popularity baseline, five seeds, per-category tables. Data: H&M.

**B. pick-profile.** Per-user preference model over generated images: learn a user vector from their past pairwise picks, predict their later picks, against a global (non-personal) preference scorer. Data: Pick-a-Pic.

**C. intake-match.** Resale intake matcher: map a photo of a returned item to its catalogue SKU, measured on held-out H&M articles (the brief's Archive reuse target).

| Criterion | A taste-rank | B pick-profile | C intake-match |
|---|---|---|---|
| Their customers feel it | **4.** The posting asks for algorithms that "model user preference and taste" and "curated feeds"; A is exactly that, but on fashion purchases, not generated images. | **5.** Same posting, and the data is real users' picks over generated images, Krea's own medium. | **1.** Nothing in Krea's product, posting or docs touches resale or SKU matching. |
| Not a clone | **4.** Krea lacks behaviour-learned taste and a per-viewer feed (section 3); no competitor read publishes a per-viewer feed ranking. Midjourney's `--p` is adjacent (generation, not ranking). Looked at: Krea changelog, docs export, blog, posting; Midjourney and Ideogram docs. | **2.** Midjourney ships behaviour-learned taste from picked images ("selecting images from a grid", Explore likes); B is that feature, measured. | **4.** Krea has nothing like it; but it is off-target for Krea (scored above). |
| Tony has proof for it | **5.** recs-engine's design is image-based taste learning with multi-index retrieval; derm is the eval method (E0, frozen split, bands). | **4.** Same taste-learning design; pairwise data is new ground. | **4.** ModSearch's local-first search is image/text retrieval. |
| Fits the budget | **4.** Public data behind a rules click and a Kaggle token; a LoRA on a ViT-B-class encoder fits the 3090; polars/DuckDB for transactions. Token is a P5 needs-tony item. | **2.** The authors' dataset repos are gone from HF (section 5); the mirror has no licence; full set ~190 GB per the README. Licence work could eat the budget. | **4.** Same data as A, smaller pipeline. |
| Measurable | **5.** Ground truth: held-out customers' purchases after the cutoff, recorded by H&M, not produced by the build. | **4.** Ground truth: users' later picks, recorded by the Pick-a-Pic app; per-user pick counts not yet checked. | **3.** H&M has one catalogue image per article, so query photos would be synthetic augmentations of the catalogue image: the build would produce part of its own ground truth. |
| **Total** | **22** | **17** | **16** |

**Pick: A, taste-rank** (22). No tie. B is the better story for Krea's medium but loses on clone risk (Midjourney ships it) and on data licence; it stays as a possible P6 transfer note, not a build.
